How to read your numbers

A good day = protein hit ✅ + calories at or a little under (0–300) ✅. Carbs and fat can move around inside that.
A good bulk day = protein hit ✅ + calories at your target (5% under to 10% over) ✅. Eating enough is the whole job.
Ring colors: ● green good · ● yellow close · ● red needs attention. Each ring shows where you'd land if the day ended right now.

Protein: the must

❌ Under: fix this one first. When you eat less, protein protects your muscle, so the weight you lose is fat, not muscle. It also keeps you full.

❌ Under: fix this one first. Protein is what new muscle is built from; the extra calories only turn into muscle if it's there.

👍 Over: fine for most people.

Goal: hit it every day, or get within about 10%.

Calories

✅ A little under: that's how you lose fat. Eating a bit less than you burn is the whole game.

⚠️ Way under (more than 25%): backfires. More hunger and binge risk, less energy, muscle loss, and slower progress. The app flags these days as TOO LOW.

❌ Over: slows fat loss. One day won't ruin anything; your week matters more than any single day.

✅ At or a little over: that's the surplus that builds muscle. Your target is already about 10% more than you burn.

⚠️ Under: no surplus, no growth. More than 15% under means you ate less than you burn, and the app flags it TOO LOW.

⚠️ Way over (more than 10%): the extra mostly turns into fat, not muscle. One day is fine; every day isn't.

⚖️ Aim to gain about 0.25–0.5% of your weight a week. The weekly report checks your pace and tells you if your target should move.

🏀 You're still growing, so your body needs the fuel. Your "too low" line is closer to your target.

Carbs

👍 Under: usually fine. Carbs are the most flexible, as long as your calories and energy are okay.

⚠️ Over: usually means calories are creeping up.

⚠️ Way under: less energy for practice and much harder to hit your calories. Rice, pasta, oats, bagels and fruit make it easy.

👍 Over: fine as long as your calories are on target. Carbs fuel the workouts that build the muscle.

Put your carbs around practice and workouts, when your body uses them best.

Fat

👍 A bit under: fine.

⚠️ Way under every day (below about 20–25% of your calories): not great for your hormones and how you feel.

⚠️ Over: the easiest way calories sneak up: fried food, sauces, dressings.
